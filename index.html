Owned by the Community, Driven by Care

Providing official stakes to community members and quality health services to Metkei.

Our journey began back in 2015 as a critical medical outreach initiative, returning annually to run free medical camps and provide treatment relief directly to the local community. This regular outreach continued faithfully until the arrival of COVID-19 in 2021, when necessary bans on public gatherings disrupted our mobile service style.

Rather than stopping, this challenge highlighted a deeper need: a permanent shelter and treatment foundation for identified patients. To fulfill this vision, German Doctors stepped forward to support our community, purchasing dedicated land and financing the construction of a permanent facility layout.

About Our Partner (German Doctors): Founded in 1983, German Doctors sends medical professionals on voluntary six-week missions to medically underserved areas globally—primarily across Kenya, India, Bangladesh, Sierra Leone, and the Philippines. Operating in severe poverty areas, refugee camps, and civil rescue environments, they provide critical basic medical care and train local healthcare personnel. To date, over 3,500 doctors have executed roughly 7,700 missions, generating over 13 million patient contacts worldwide.

Construction moved forward seamlessly, culminating in the completion of our modern hospital framework by January 2025. Today, Metkei Community Health Centre is fully operational—built for the community, run by the community, and managed locally by our dedicated directors, continuing our core tradition of targeted local outreach.

AMPATH

AMPATH works closely with our health centre to provide management expertise for chronic health conditions including HIV, Hypertension, and Diabetes. Their revolving pharmacy system guarantees that essential medications remain continually stocked and accessible to our community patients.

German Doctors

German Doctors funded our structural baseline project from the ground up, purchasing the physical land plot and funding the construction of our main Outpatient Department (OPD) clinic rooms, paving the way for our transition into a permanent treatment center.

University of California, San Francisco (UCSF)

The University of California, San Francisco is a prestigious public land-grant research university dedicated entirely to health science and life science. Founded as Toland Medical College in 1864 and affiliated with the University of California system since 1873, UCSF stands as a powerhouse of medical exploration. In 2021, the institution spent $1.71 billion in advanced research and development, receiving the second-highest research funding allocation from the National Institutes of Health (NIH). UCSF's elite medical faculty have treated patients and trained healthcare residents for over a century, enriching our community medical programs with global healthcare research depth.

County Government

The County Department of Health delivers coordinating policy leadership and healthcare guidance while supplying our institution with vital diagnostic tools, child health vaccines, electronic tracking systems, and routine clinical oversight.

The Community

Metkei residents constitute the true owners and foundational pillars of our center. The community directly votes for and supplies members of our Governing Board of Directors, keeping local needs at the absolute core of our daily operations.

Primary Medical Care

General Outpatients services, full-service pharmacy dispensing, and laboratory diagnostics.

Maternal & Child Health

Comprehensive Antenatal Care (ANC) for pregnant mothers, safe Maternity services, and routine infant immunization.

HIV & Care Programs

Prevention of Mother-to-Child Transmission (PMTCT) services and dedicated drug dispensing for HIV-positive patients.

Specialized & Community Care

Point-of-Care Ultrasound diagnostics and active community health outreach via Community Health Promoter (CHPs) services.

Free Medical Camps

Organizing strategic, zero-cost medical outreach camps to deliver specialized checkups, screening, and diagnostic relief directly to community doorsteps.
